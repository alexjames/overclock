Shared Responsiblity Principle

The Shared Responsiblity Model draws a line between what a cloud provider secures and what the customer secures.
Typically, the provider is responsbile for security "of" the cloud and the customer is responsible for securing infrastructure "in" the cloud.
For instance, the security of the physical host and hypervisor is the sole Responsiblity of the provider.
Application code, IAM and data classification are the responsiblity of the customer.
Thus things like misconfigured S3 buckets, overly permissive IAM policies and weak database passwords fall on the customer.
